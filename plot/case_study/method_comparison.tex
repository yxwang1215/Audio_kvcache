But that plural marriage is a vital tenet of the church is not true. Yet the two have often been confused in the popular mind. \textbf{Analysis} \\
\hline
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{SnapKV} suffers from severe repetition failure: After the phrase ``We believe in a literal resurrection, and in an actual hereafter, in which future states shall recognize'', the model enters an infinite loop, repeating ``every future state shall recognize'' hundreds of times, resulting in a WER of 93.62\%.
    \item \textbf{SparseKV-FFT} successfully completes the transcription with high accuracy (WER: 1.09\%), correctly transcribing the entire passage including the complex sentence about resurrection and marriage beliefs.
    \item The repetition issue in SnapKV demonstrates the importance of maintaining long-range context in KV-cache compression methods for long-form audio transcription tasks.
    \item SparseKV-FFT's FFT-based compression strategy better preserves the contextual information necessary for coherent long-form generation.
\end{itemize}
