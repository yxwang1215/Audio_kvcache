    \paragraph{Response to ovZo-Q5.}
    We thank the reviewer for pointing out that the 0.0 entries in Table~1 need more explicit metric documentation.
    We agree that the current text is too brief: a zero score should not be read as a missing result or an uncomputed metric.
    In the revision, we will clarify that Table~1 reports bounded task accuracy, and that for error-rate metrics we convert raw WER/CER to accuracy by clipping at the lower bound:
    \[
        \mathrm{Acc}_{\mathrm{WER}}=\max(0, 100-\mathrm{WER}), \qquad
        \mathrm{Acc}_{\mathrm{CER}}=\max(0, 100-\mathrm{CER}).
    \]
    Therefore, a displayed 0.0 means that the raw error rate reached or exceeded 100\%, or that an answer was judged incorrect for QA-style tasks; it does \emph{not} mean that the sample was dropped, that the metric failed to run, or that an empty placeholder was inserted.
